A decade in motion

Started small.
Built to last.

Every chapter is a decision to make training more considered, more human, and more worth showing up for.

2012

The first rep

One rack.
One standard.

Stackly opened in a 900-square-foot garage with a handful of athletes and a promise to coach the person, not just the movement.

2015

The room grows

More space.
Same focus.

Our first studio opened in Salem, giving the community room to lift heavier and stay longer.

2018

The full picture

Strength met
recovery.

Mobility, breathwork, and recovery joined the program. Performance became a longer game, not a louder one.

2024

The next chapter

A bigger room
for better work.

Today, thousands train at Stackly. The equipment is better, the team is deeper, and the original standard is still the one that matters.

What moves us
